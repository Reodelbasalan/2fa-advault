Three steps, then never again

1

Grab your setup key

In any account's security settings, choose “Authenticator app”, then tap Can't scan it? to reveal the setup key.

2

Paste it into the Vault

Spaces and lower case are fine. The preview shows your code instantly — check it against the account, then save.

3

Tap to copy, forever

The code sits on a card, refreshing every 30 seconds. One tap copies it. No more typing 32-character secrets on every login.

Why not just use a website?

Most online 2FA tools ask you to paste your secret into their page. That secret isn't a password — it is the second factor, permanently, for every future login on that account. Once it leaves your machine, you can't know what happened to it.

This never sends it anywhere. There's no server to trust and no request to intercept. The code is proof: it's running right now, in this tab, with the internet doing nothing.
